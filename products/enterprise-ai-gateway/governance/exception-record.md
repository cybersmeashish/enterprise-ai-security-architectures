# Time-bounded exception

**Synthetic example EX-SAMPLE-01:** local HTTP only in the network-isolated fixture. Owner and independent approver are synthetic roles; scope excludes real data and remote access; expiry 1 January 2027.

Record actual requirement, necessity, affected workload/data, scope, owner, independent approver, compensating control, expiry, evidence, review and removal plan. Do not broaden an exception through a retry or fallback.

The example validator rejects expired or self-approved exceptions. Passing validation does not establish that a real risk owner accepted the exception.
