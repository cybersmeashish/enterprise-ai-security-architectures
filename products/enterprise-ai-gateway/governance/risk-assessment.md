# AI risk assessment and treatment

**Synthetic example:** R01 indirect injection. Threat actor supplies malicious retrieval text; exposed assets are answer integrity and action authority. Existing mitigation is document ACL plus a tool authority boundary outside the model.

Residual issue: the model may still follow injected instructions. Treatment: representative learned-model evaluation and source trust/ingestion controls before real deployment. Accountable roles: security, data and business process owner.

For each risk record cause, affected people/assets, likelihood basis, consequence, mitigation, evidence, uncertainty, residual acceptance, owner and next review. Avoid fabricated risk scores when evidence is absent.
