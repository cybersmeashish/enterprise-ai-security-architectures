# Audit, retention and evidence custody record

**Synthetic example:** metadata-only local audit; encrypted response replay with 30-second logical expiry. Tail deletion is not detected without an external checkpoint.

Specify record purpose, fields, sensitivity, owner, access roles, location, integrity anchor, expected sequence, clock confidence, retention, deletion/backup propagation, export recipient, custody and incident hold.

Separate logical expiry, application deletion, backup expiry and physical erasure. Verify each with evidence. A hash chain is not a claim of complete or administrator-proof evidence.
