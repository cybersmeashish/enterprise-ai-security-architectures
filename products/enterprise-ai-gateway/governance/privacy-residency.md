# Privacy, residency and applicability worksheet

**Synthetic example:** government procedure text; no personal records; declared local site; no external provider route. This does not establish jurisdiction compliance.

Identify data subject, purpose, source authority, legal/contractual reviewer, organizational role, affected locations, inference, retrieval, logs, caches, backups, keys, administrators, support, transfers and deletion. Separate declarations from observed evidence.

Determine jurisdiction and sector applicability through an accountable qualified reviewer. Record uncertainty, source, date and decision. Do not turn a framework mapping into an automatic legal classification.
