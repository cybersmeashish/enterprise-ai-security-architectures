# ADR 0003 — AI governance and bank accountability

**Status:** adopted for the reference; production implementation/acceptance pending.

**Owners:** Business, AI governance, legal and risk.

## Context

A century-old archive and a vendor score do not establish lawful use, current applicability or an acceptable material-error rate.

## Decision

Approve purpose, dataset rights, risk tier, model limitations and acceptance evidence through named owners before onboarding.

## Alternatives and implications

Vendor self-certification or a generic AI checklist cannot substitute owner acceptance.

## Evidence required

Pin entity applicability and commencement; produce reviewed risk/evaluation/change records with unresolved gaps.

The current repository establishes design intent and explicitly bounded lab evidence. It does not establish this decision's implementation in a bank.
