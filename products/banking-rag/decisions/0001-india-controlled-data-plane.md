# ADR 0001 — India-controlled data plane

**Status:** adopted for the reference; production implementation/acceptance pending.

**Owners:** Platform, legal and CISO.

## Context

A region label does not constrain operator, support, subprocessor or backup access. The bank adopts this boundary pending legal/entity applicability, not as a universal localisation claim.

## Decision

Keep originals, derivatives, inference, operators and recovery in approved Indian boundaries, with explicit export routes.

## Alternatives and implications

Unreviewed global hosted processing; bank on-premises or approved India cloud remain possible after evidence.

## Evidence required

Inventory and observe every data/authority exit, support route and restore placement.

The current repository establishes design intent and explicitly bounded lab evidence. It does not establish this decision's implementation in a bank.
